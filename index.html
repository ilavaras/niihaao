01 A learner first

慢慢来，比较快。

Years of quiet practice are becoming a new possibility.

I began Chinese as a personal passion. With steady practice over several years, unfamiliar sounds became recognisable, characters began to carry meaning, and HSK4 content became comfortable.

Now the journey is changing. The next chapter is about active Mandarin—speaking spontaneously, writing clearly and eventually helping other Indian learners find their own way into the language.

“I am not presenting myself as someone who has finished learning. I am building from the honest experience of someone who stayed with it.”
